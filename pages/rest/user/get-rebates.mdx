---
title: 'Get rebates'
description: 'Your maker rebate rate, totals, and one row per rebate credit'
---

Returns your **maker rebate rate**, your rebate totals, and the rebates credited to your account, one row per fill.

A rebate is paid to the **maker** of a matched offer when the game is graded. See [Fees](/pages/fees#maker-rebate) for the rate and how the amount is calculated.

## Request

`GET /user/getRebates`

Authentication is required (see [Authentication](/pages/rest/authentication)).

<ParamField query="sinceDays" type="integer">
  Return only rebates credited in the last N days, up to `365`. Omit it to get your full history. `summary` is not affected by this parameter.
</ParamField>

<CodeGroup>
```bash curl
curl "https://api.4casters.io/user/getRebates?sinceDays=30" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN"
```
</CodeGroup>

## Response

<ResponseField name="data.rebateRate" type="number">
  Your rebate rate as a fraction: `0.001` is 0.1%. `null` when your account earns no rebate. This is your prematch rate, or your live rate if you only have a live one.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="data.rebateRates" type="object">
  Your rate per mode. Each is a fraction, or `null` when you earn no rebate in that mode.
  <Expandable title="rebateRates">
    <ResponseField name="prematch" type="number">Rate on prematch games.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="live" type="number">Rate on live games.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="data.summary" type="object">
  Totals across your whole history, whatever `sinceDays` is.
  <Expandable title="summary">
    <ResponseField name="totalEarned" type="number">All rebates ever credited.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="last7Days" type="number">Rebates credited in the last 7 days.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="last30Days" type="number">Rebates credited in the last 30 days.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="data.rebates" type="array">
  Rebate credits, newest first. At most the newest **5,000** rows are returned per request; use `sinceDays` to keep the list short.
  <Expandable title="Rebate">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">Id of the rebate credit.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="txID" type="string">Id of the fill the rebate was paid on. The same `txID` as on [Get matched bets](/pages/rest/user/get-matched-bets) and [Get graded bets](/pages/rest/user/get-graded). `null` on rebates credited before October 2026.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="orderID" type="string">Id of your order that was filled.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="gameID" type="string">Id of the game.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="amount" type="number">Amount credited to your balance.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string">ISO 8601 time the rebate was credited, which is when the fill was graded.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="game" type="object">`eventName`, `league` and `start` of the game. `null` if the game can't be loaded.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="type" type="string">`moneyline`, `spread`, `total`, or `moneyline1x2`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="odds" type="number">American odds from your side.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="side" type="string">The side you had, as text: a team name, a team and spread, or `Over` / `Under` and the total. `null` for `moneyline1x2`.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Rebates on graded bets

The rebate on a single fill is also on that fill's row in [Get graded bets](/pages/rest/user/get-graded) and [Get graded bets since a cursor](/pages/rest/user/get-graded-since), as `rebate`.
