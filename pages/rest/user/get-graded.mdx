Returns your **graded (settled) bets** on games that started inside a date window, plus a P&L and volume summary.

Use this endpoint for reports over a known period ("everything on games last week"). To keep a local copy of your history in sync as bets settle, use [Get graded bets since a cursor](/pages/rest/user/get-graded-since) instead — it walks settlements in order, so you never have to guess a date window.

## How rows work

- **One row per fill.** An order that matched three times comes back as three rows, each with its own `txID`. Use `txID` as the row's identity.
- **Both sides of the book.** Orders you took appear with `origin: "wager"`; orders you posted that someone took appear with `origin: "offer"`. Either way `bet` and `odds` are from **your** side.
- **Dates match the game's start time,** not the settlement time. A bet that settles late still falls in its game's window.
- **The game is embedded** in every row.

## Request

`GET /user/v2/getGraded`

The same parameters can also be sent as a JSON body with `POST /user/v2/getGraded`.

<ParamField query="startDate" type="string">
  Start of the window, inclusive, as any ISO 8601 timestamp, matched against the game start time. Omit it to get your **full history** — the 35-day limit below does not apply.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="endDate" type="string">
  End of the window, exclusive, as any ISO 8601 timestamp. Defaults to now.
</ParamField>

<Warning>
When you send `startDate`, the window from `startDate` to `endDate` may be **at most 35 days**. A wider window is rejected with `400` — it is never silently truncated. Page through longer periods in 35-day steps.
</Warning>

<CodeGroup>
```bash curl
curl "https://api.4casters.io/user/v2/getGraded?startDate=2026-08-01T00:00:00.000Z&endDate=2026-08-08T00:00:00.000Z" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN"
```
</CodeGroup>

<ResponseField name="data.summary" type="object">
  Totals over the returned rows.
  <Expandable title="summary">
    <ResponseField name="pnl" type="number">Sum of `result`, in whole dollars.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="volume" type="number">Sum of the smaller of `risk` and `win` per row, in whole dollars.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>

<Note>
Money fields on rows (`fee`, `risk`, `win`, `result`) are **strings** with two decimals, so they add up exactly. The `summary` totals are rounded numbers.
</Note>

In rare cases a row's order can't be loaded. That row carries only `txID`, `ticketNumber`, `settledAt`, `matchedTime` and `game`, and its `id` is an object `{ "_id": "…", "error": "…" }` instead of a string. Skip or log it.

## Rate limits

**60 requests per minute per user**, on top of the [global per-IP limit](/pages/rate-limiting#per-ip-rest). A `429` carries a `Retry-After` header in seconds.

## Errors

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `400` | Malformed date, or a window wider than 35 days. |
| `401` | Missing or invalid auth token. |
| `403` | Your account may not use this endpoint. |
| `429` | Rate limited. Wait `Retry-After` seconds. |
