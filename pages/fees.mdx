## Who pays

Commission is charged to the **taker** on each matched bet. Makers pay no commission.

There is no fee to place, edit or cancel an order, to use the API, or on a bet that pushes or is refunded.

## Your rate

Your rate is set per account. It is `commissionCharged` on [Get current user](/pages/rest/user/get-me) and in the [login response](/pages/rest/authentication), as a fraction: `0.01` is 1%. The standard rate is 1%.

Accounts with over $2M in taker volume in a month can be moved to 0.75% taker commission for the following month.

## Calculation

`toWin` is your stake × (decimal odds − 1).

| Result | Commission |
|---|---|
| Win | rate × `toWin` |
| Loss | rate × the smaller of stake and `toWin` |
| Half win or half loss (quarter lines) | half of the above |
| Push or refund | none |

On a favorite you pay rate × `toWin` either way. On an underdog you pay rate × `toWin` if you win and rate × stake if you lose.

Examples at 1%:

| Bet | If it wins | If it loses |
|---|---|---|
| $100 at -200 (to win $50) | $0.50 fee, you win $49.50 | $0.50 fee, you lose $100.50 |
| $100 at +150 (to win $150) | $1.50 fee, you win $148.50 | $1.00 fee, you lose $101.00 |

## Maker rebate

Makers are paid a rebate on each of their offers that is matched.

Your rate is `rebateRate` on [Get current user](/pages/rest/user/get-me), as a fraction: `0.001` is 0.1%. It is `null` when your account earns no rebate. Every account earns 0.1% on fills matched before 2027-01-01 05:00 UTC (midnight ET on January 1).

The rebate on a fill is rate × the smaller of the stake and `toWin`, whether your side wins or loses. A half win or half loss pays half. A push or refund pays none.

It is credited to your balance when the game is graded, one credit per fill. [Get rebates](/pages/rest/user/get-rebates) lists the credits and your totals, and `rebate` on [Get graded bets](/pages/rest/user/get-graded) is the rebate on a single fill.

## When it is charged

Commission is charged when the bet is graded. A regrade reverses it and charges it again on the new result.

Before grading it is already inside `risk` and `win`: `risk` is your stake plus the commission on a loss, and `win` is your winnings minus the commission on a win. See [Get matched bets](/pages/rest/user/get-matched-bets) and the [user feed](/pages/streaming/user-feed).

After grading, `fee` is the amount charged. See [Get graded bets](/pages/rest/user/get-graded).
